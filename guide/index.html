How to use a trading journal: step-by-step guide

Updated October 31, 2026

Short answer: To use a trading journal well: set your risk rules, log every trade right after entry, tag mistakes and emotions right after exit, review daily against your limits, and review weekly to choose one mistake to fix. This guide shows each step in EdgeTrace.

Steps

  1. Set your account and risk rules. Open Settings and enter your starting balance, maximum risk per trade (for example 1%), daily loss limit and maximum trades per day.
  2. Log or import your trades. Press N or click New Trade to log a trade, or import a CSV file or MT4/MT5 account history from Import & Export. Leave the exit empty while a trade is open.
  3. Tag the behaviour behind each trade. After the exit, tag the setup, any mistakes, your emotion, whether you followed your plan and an execution grade, and write one lesson.
  4. Check your guardrails daily. Before and during the session, check the Overview for today’s P&L, trades taken and loss limit. Stop when the "Stop trading for today" banner appears.
  5. Review weekly and fix one thing. Open Analytics, read the Coach’s Action Plan and the Mistakes Over Time chart, and pick the top mistake as your focus for the next 20 trades.

What should I record for every trade?

  • Symbol, date, entry time, side (long/short), entry, exit, quantity and fees.
  • Stop loss and take profit – required for risk, R-multiple and planned risk:reward.
  • Point value for futures/forex, or the broker’s net P&L.
  • Setup, mistakes, emotion, whether you followed your plan and an execution grade.
  • A short note: why you took it and what you would do differently.

How do I import trades from MT4 or MT5?

In MetaTrader, open the account history, right-click and save it as a report, then export or copy it as CSV with the columns Ticket, Open Time, Type, Size, Item, Price, S/L, T/P, Close Time, Price, Commission, Swap and Profit. In EdgeTrace, open Import & Export and drop the file. Duplicate tickets are skipped, so you can re-import a growing history safely.

What CSV format does EdgeTrace accept?

Columns (any order): date, time, pair or symbol, side, quantity, multiplier, entry, exit, stopLoss, takeProfit, fees, netPnl, grade, notes, tags, mistakes, emotions, followedPlan, screenshot. Separate multiple tags or mistakes with a vertical bar (|). A ready-made template can be downloaded in the app.

How often should I review my trading journal?

Briefly after every trade (to tag it honestly), at the end of each day (to check rules and write one lesson) and in depth once a week (to choose the next mistake to fix and confirm the previous one is fading).
