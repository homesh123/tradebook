EdgeTrace – free trading journal that finds and fixes your trading mistakes

EdgeTrace is a free, private trading journal for stocks, forex, futures and crypto. Log trades, tag mistakes and emotions, and get analytics that show what each mistake costs you and exactly how to fix it.

What EdgeTrace does

  • Ranks every trading mistake by how much money it cost you, with a rule and fix for each.
  • Shows your equity curve next to a "disciplined" curve: your P&L if you had skipped every mistake trade.
  • Detects tilt and revenge trading by comparing the trade after a loss with the trade after a win.
  • Analyses results by emotion, setup, day of week and entry hour.
  • Enforces your risk rules: max risk per trade, daily loss limit and max trades per day.
  • Imports CSV and MetaTrader 4/5 history; exports CSV and JSON backups. Free, no sign-up, private.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best free trading journal?

The best free trading journal is one you will use after every trade and that turns your data into decisions. EdgeTrace is free, private (data stays in your browser) and focuses on behaviour: it ranks your mistakes by cost, detects tilt and gives you a prioritized fix plan.

Do I need to create an account to use EdgeTrace?

No. EdgeTrace works instantly in the browser with no sign-up. Your data is stored locally on your device.

Can I import trades from MetaTrader 5?

Yes. Export your account history from MT4 or MT5 as CSV and import it. EdgeTrace uses broker profit plus commission and swap as the exact net P&L and skips duplicate tickets.

Is EdgeTrace free?

Yes. EdgeTrace is completely free and requires no account.

Where is my trading data stored?

Only in your own browser’s local storage. EdgeTrace has no server and never uploads your trades. Clearing browser data deletes them, so download a backup regularly.

Which markets can I journal?

Stocks, ETFs, options, futures, forex and crypto – anything with an entry, exit and size. Use the point-value multiplier for futures and forex, or enter the broker’s net P&L.

How is P&L calculated?

P&L = (exit − entry) × quantity × multiplier − fees, reversed for short trades. If you enter the broker’s net P&L, that exact value is used instead.
