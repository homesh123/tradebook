Frequently asked questions

Is EdgeTrace free?

Yes. EdgeTrace is completely free and requires no account.

Where is my trading data stored?

Only in your own browser’s local storage. EdgeTrace has no server and never uploads your trades. Clearing browser data deletes them, so download a backup regularly.

Which markets can I journal?

Stocks, ETFs, options, futures, forex and crypto – anything with an entry, exit and size. Use the point-value multiplier for futures and forex, or enter the broker’s net P&L.

How is P&L calculated?

P&L = (exit − entry) × quantity × multiplier − fees, reversed for short trades. If you enter the broker’s net P&L, that exact value is used instead.

How is the R-multiple calculated?

R = (exit − entry) ÷ (entry − stop loss), reversed for shorts. It is price based, so it stays correct for any contract size.

Can I import from MetaTrader 4 or 5?

Yes. Import an MT4/MT5 history CSV; broker profit plus commission and swap is used as net P&L and duplicate tickets are skipped.

Can I export my data?

Yes – as CSV for spreadsheets, or as a JSON backup that includes trades, settings and custom tags and can be restored on another device.

What is the discipline score?

A 0–100 score combining the share of mistake-free trades (60%) and plan adherence (40%). 75+ is disciplined, 50–74 inconsistent, below 50 undisciplined.

Why don’t I see suggestions yet?

You need at least three closed trades. Suggestions improve as you tag mistakes, emotions and plan adherence consistently.

Does EdgeTrace work offline?

Once loaded, the app runs in your browser and stores data locally, so journaling does not depend on a server.
